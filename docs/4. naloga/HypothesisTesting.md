Task 1:
- Perform interview with 3-5 people who are potential users of your solution
- Keep a record of their answers
- In the document be sure to include:
    - Who are the people you spoke with
    - The questions you asked
    - The answers you received
    - Conclusions (are your hypotheses OK, is there anything you need to change?)

Task 2:
- Based on the conclusions from task 1, fix/complete the prototype you have made to be ready for the usability testing
- (if possible) Include the link for the prototype

Notes:
I recommend you to perform offline interviews, record the answers and then manually transcribe them. It is tedious, but a very useful exercise.
