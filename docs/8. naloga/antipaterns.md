## 2. Negative paterns:

- Bad delegation of tasks
	- It represented a bottleneck for productivity since a lot of comunication was just back and forth with not clear task definitions and delegation.

- Compromise the Hiring Bar
	- At the begginig we assigned the roles in a rushed non-systematic way. The consequence of this was that everybody did a bit of everything and there was no clear distinction between the roles. 


- Lack of clear deadlines
	- A lot of the time we have not organised timely mettings and set clear goals and deadlines. As a result we rushed some development stages.



## 3. Positive Paterns

- Be a Zen Master
	- A very chill work enviroment enabled us to do the work with enjoyoment and no tension between the coworkers.

- Be Honest
	- The promotion of honesty allowed us to easily brainsorm and iterate on ideas to develope them to a higher level of quality.

- Be a Catalyst
	- Self inititive of individuals served as a catalyst for the whole group to join in and push the task forward.
